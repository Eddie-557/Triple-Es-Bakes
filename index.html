About Triple Es Bakes

Triple Es Bakes is a Nigerian bakery and small-chops kitchen in Kubwa, Abuja. We started with a simple idea: food from a bakery should feel like it came from a warm, welcoming home.

We bake and fry in small batches, so what reaches your table is fresh. We keep our recipes familiar and generous because the best celebrations are built on food people actually enjoy.

Quality
Good ingredients and careful hands in every bake.
Freshness
Prepared in small batches and ready when you are.
Your satisfaction
We listen, we adjust, and we want you to come back.
